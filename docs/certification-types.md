# Certification Types

## Overview

FreeCerts classifies certifications into distinct types to help users understand the nature and value of each credential.

## Credential Types

### `free_certificate`

The course and certificate are completely free. The learning material, assessment, and certificate are all provided at no cost.

**Examples:**
- freeCodeCamp certifications
- Harvard CS50 free certificates
- Kaggle Learn completion certificates

**Characteristics:**
- 100% free learning
- Free assessment (exam, project, or quiz)
- Free digital certificate
- No credit card required

### `free_professional_certification`

The certification and required assessment or exam are free. These are industry-recognized credentials from professional organizations or technology companies.

**Examples:**
- Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Foundations
- HubSpot professional certifications
- Cisco Networking Academy certifications

**Characteristics:**
- Professional-level credential
- Formal assessment or exam
- Industry recognition
- Often aligned with job roles

### `free_course_certificate`

A free educational course awards a certificate, but it is not necessarily an industry-recognized professional certification.

**Examples:**
- Kaggle Learn micro-courses
- AWS digital training badges
- Microsoft Learn completion badges
- IBM SkillsBuild badges

**Characteristics:**
- Free learning and certificate
- May not be industry-recognized
- Often shorter in duration
- Focus on specific skills

### `free_training_paid_exam`

Training is free, but the certification examination costs money. These entries are tracked separately and do NOT appear in the primary free-certification directory.

**Examples:**
- Many vendor certifications where training is free but the exam is paid

**Characteristics:**
- Free learning materials
- Paid exam or assessment
- Professional certification available at cost

### `financial_aid_or_scholarship`

Free access depends on financial aid, a scholarship, or an application. These are kept separate from universally free options.

**Examples:**
- Coursera courses with financial aid
- edX verified certificates with aid

**Characteristics:**
- Application required
- Not universally available
- May have eligibility requirements

### `free_trial_only`

Access depends on a limited free trial. These are excluded from the genuinely free directory.

**Examples:**
- Platforms offering 7-30 day free trials with paid continuation

**Characteristics:**
- Time-limited access
- Requires payment after trial
- Not genuinely free

## Distinction Between Credentials

### Course Completion Certificate

Awarded for completing a course, may include a final assessment. Focuses on participation and completion.

**Example:** Kaggle Learn certificates, freeCodeCamp certifications.

### Professional Certification

Industry-recognized credential awarded after passing a formal exam or assessment. Validates specific job-ready skills.

**Example:** Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Foundations, HubSpot certifications.

### Digital Badge

Visual credential (often via Credly or Acclaim) representing a specific skill or achievement. Can be shared on LinkedIn and resumes.

**Example:** IBM SkillsBuild badges, MongoDB skill badges.

### Attendance Certificate

Certificate for participating in or completing a course, not necessarily assessing competency.

**Example:** Some webinar or event completion certificates.

## Choosing the Right Type

When submitting a certification:

1. **Is the certificate industry-recognized?** → `free_professional_certification`
2. **Is it a free course with a certificate?** → `free_course_certificate`
3. **Is it a free verified certification like freeCodeCamp?** → `free_certificate`
4. **Is training free but the exam paid?** → `free_training_paid_exam` (excluded from primary directory)
5. **Does it require financial aid?** → `financial_aid_or_scholarship`
6. **Is it only available via free trial?** → `free_trial_only` (excluded)

## Additional Fields

Use the `notes` field to clarify:

- Whether the credential is a badge, certificate, or both
- The issuing authority or platform
- Any renewal or recertification requirements
- Geographic or eligibility restrictions
